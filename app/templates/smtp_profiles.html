SMTP Configuration Help
Gmail
  • Enable 2-Factor Authentication
  • Generate an App Password
  • Use App Password (not your regular password)
  • Server: smtp.gmail.com, Port: 587
Outlook/Office 365
  • Enable SMTP AUTH in admin settings
  • May need App Password with 2FA
  • Server: smtp.office365.com, Port: 587
  • Use your full email as username
Best Practices
  • Start with low daily limits (warmup)
  • Use dedicated sending domains
  • Configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC
  • Monitor bounce rates closely
